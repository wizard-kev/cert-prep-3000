Study

Practice

Feedback after every answer. Leans toward your weak objectives, brings back questions you missed once enough time has passed, and keeps introducing objectives you haven't tried.

Exam simulation

Timed, like the real exam, with no feedback until the end. Mark questions to come back to, and report problems without stopping. Prefers questions you haven't seen.

Question pool

How questions are checked

New questions are Unreviewed: they passed the app's quality checks. Once you answer one without reporting it, it becomes Passively-Validated. Choose Looks right after an answer to mark it Validated. Report a problem takes a question out of play at once (Disputed); you then confirm the report, which retires it, or undo it.

Work with Claude

Get new questions, or a guided study session, from Claude in a web chat. The app writes the prompt; you paste it into claude.ai and bring Claude's questions back here.
